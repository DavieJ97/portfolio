01 / A little about me

Developer at heart.
Builder by curiosity.

I’m Dawid De Lange—a self-taught Python developer and creator. My path into software began in solving day to day issues. I looked at the world around me and thought how can i make life easier. That's when I saw how much time teachers lost to manual lesson prep and fragmented tools.

Wanting to solve those everyday problems, I taught myself Python, database management, and interactive web development. That journey became TeachersOnline—a production SaaS platform designed to help teachers create engaging, gamified lessons with less friction.

I enjoy working where backend logic, user experience, and practical software design meet. Away from shipping features and refining data models, I’m usually learning something new, experimenting with code, or looking for ways technology can make a complex workflow feel simple.

✳ Former educator ⌘ Self-taught developer ↗ EdTech creator
